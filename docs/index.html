Understanding Key Growth Factors

Nitrogen (N)

Essential for leaf and stem growth (vegetative growth). It is a core component of chlorophyll, giving plants their green color.

Phosphorus (P)

Crucial for strong root development, seed germination, and the transfer of energy within the plant. Aids in flowering and fruiting.

Potassium (K)

Improves overall plant vigor, disease resistance, and water regulation. Essential for producing high-quality fruit and grain.

Soil pH

Affects the availability of all other nutrients. Most crops thrive in a neutral range (6.0 to 7.5) where nutrients are most accessible.

Temperature

Dictates the rate of plant growth and metabolic processes. Each crop has an optimal temperature range for germination and growth.

Soil Type

Determines water retention and nutrient-holding capacity. Different crops are adapted to different soil structures, from sandy to clay.

Rice (Oryza sativa)

Temp: 20–35°C Soil pH: 5.5–6.5 Water: High / standing

Climate & Soil

  • Tropical/subtropical; thrives with abundant water or assured irrigation.
  • Clayey to loamy soils with high water-holding capacity; pH 5.5–6.5 ideal.

Land Preparation

  • Puddling (flood + till) to reduce percolation and suppress weeds; laser levelling improves uniformity.

Seed & Sowing

  • Use region-specific high-yielding/resistant varieties. Transplant 20–25 day-old seedlings (short-duration).

Nutrient Management

  • N: 100–150 kg/ha split: basal, tillering, panicle initiation. P: 40–60; K: 40–60 kg/ha.
  • Apply ZnSO4 ~25 kg/ha where zinc deficiency is common.

Irrigation

  • Maintain 2–5 cm standing water till flowering; drain before harvest.

Weeds, Pests & Diseases

  • Pests: stem borer, brown planthopper, leaf folder. Diseases: blast, sheath blight, bacterial blight.
  • Adopt IPM: resistant varieties, proper spacing, timely sprays, field sanitation.

Harvest & Yield

  • Harvest at 20–25% grain moisture; expected yield 4–6 t/ha (up to 8–10 t/ha with improved practices).

Maize (Zea mays)

Temp: 18–27°C Soil pH: 5.5–7.5 Water: 500–800 mm

Climate & Soil

  • Warm-season crop; well-drained loams preferred.

Land Preparation

  • Fine tilth; ridge–furrow in heavy soils for drainage.

Seed & Sowing

  • Hybrids; spacing rows 60–75 cm, plants 20–25 cm.

Nutrient Management

  • N: 120–180 kg/ha in 3–4 splits (basal, knee-high, tasseling, grain fill). P: 60–80; K: 40–60 kg/ha.
  • Zn/Boron in deficient soils improves cob filling.

Irrigation

  • Critical: knee-high, tasseling, grain filling. Avoid waterlogging.

Weeds, Pests & Diseases

  • Pests: fall armyworm, stem borer; Diseases: downy mildew, leaf blight.
  • Early weed control (first 30 days) crucial.

Harvest & Yield

  • Harvest at 20–25% kernel moisture; dry to 12–14% for storage. Yield 5–7 t/ha (up to 10 t/ha).

Chickpea (Cicer arietinum)

Temp: 10–25°C Soil pH: 6.0–7.5 Rainfed friendly

Climate & Soil

  • Cool-season pulse; avoid waterlogging; sandy loam to clay loam.

Land Preparation

  • One deep plough + harrowing; raised beds in heavy soils.

Seed & Sowing

  • Wilt-resistant varieties; rows 30–45 cm, depth 5–8 cm.

Nutrient Management

  • Starter N 20–25 kg/ha; P 40–60 kg/ha; K only if deficient. Use Rhizobium inoculation.

Irrigation

  • Mostly rainfed; 1–2 irrigations at flowering & pod fill if possible.

Weeds, Pests & Diseases

  • Pests: pod borer; Diseases: Fusarium wilt, Ascochyta blight. Rotate with cereals.

Harvest & Yield

  • Harvest when 90% pods brown; 1.5–2 t/ha rainfed; 2.5–3.5 t/ha irrigated.

Kidney Beans (Phaseolus vulgaris)

Temp: Mild Soil pH: 6.0–7.0 Drainage critical

Climate & Soil

  • Temperate/subtropical; avoid frost/heat extremes; loamy, well-drained soils.

Land Preparation

  • Fine, weed-free seedbed; raised beds improve drainage.

Seed & Sowing

  • Spacing rows 30–45 cm; plants 8–10 cm.

Nutrient Management

  • Starter N 20–25 kg/ha; P 40–60; K ~40 kg/ha if deficient. Avoid excess N (delays flowering).

Irrigation

  • Critical at flowering & pod fill; avoid waterlogging.

Weeds, Pests & Diseases

  • Pests: aphids, bean fly; Diseases: anthracnose, rust, root rots. Seed treatment helps.

Harvest & Yield

  • Harvest dry pods when mature; 1.5–2.5 t/ha dry beans.

Pigeon Peas (Cajanus cajan)

Temp: 18–30°C Soil pH: 5.0–8.0 Drought tolerant

Climate & Soil

  • Semi-arid suited; well-drained sandy loams best.

Land Preparation

  • Deep plough + harrow; ensure drainage.

Seed & Sowing

  • Sow early/medium/late types as per rainfall; spacing 60–90 cm.

Nutrient Management

  • Starter N 20–25 kg/ha; P 50–60; K 30–40 kg/ha if deficient; fixes N via Rhizobium.

Irrigation

  • Mostly rainfed; one irrigation at pod fill boosts yield.

Weeds, Pests & Diseases

  • Pests: pod borer; Diseases: wilt, sterility mosaic. Intercrop with cereals; use resistant varieties.

Harvest & Yield

  • Harvest at ~80% pod maturity; 1–1.5 t/ha rainfed; 2–2.5 t/ha irrigated.

Moth Bean (Vigna aconitifolia)

Temp: 25–35°C Soil pH: 6.0–7.5 Arid adapted

Climate & Soil

  • Excellent drought tolerance; sandy/sandy loam soils; performs on low fertility.

Land Preparation

  • Minimal tillage; one deep plough + 1–2 harrowings.

Seed & Sowing

  • Spacing 30–45 cm × 10–15 cm; depth 3–4 cm.

Nutrient Management

  • Starter N 15–20 kg/ha; P 30–40 kg/ha; K rarely needed unless deficient.

Irrigation

  • Usually rainfed; if irrigated, water at flowering & pod fill only.

Weeds, Pests & Diseases

  • Pests: pod borer, aphids; Diseases: YMV, powdery mildew. Use resistant varieties; seed treatment.

Harvest & Yield

  • Harvest when pods brown; 0.5–1.0 t/ha rainfed; up to 1.5 t/ha irrigated.

Mung Bean (Vigna radiata)

Temp: 25–35°C Soil pH: 6.2–7.2 Short duration

Climate & Soil

  • Tropical/subtropical; sensitive to waterlogging; 60–70 day maturity (variety dependent).

Land Preparation

  • Deep plough + harrow; clean, weed-free bed.

Seed & Sowing

  • Row spacing 30–45 cm; plant spacing 10–15 cm; depth 3–5 cm.

Nutrient Management

  • Starter N 15–20 kg/ha; P 40–50 kg/ha; K rarely required. Rhizobium inoculation recommended.

Irrigation

  • Kharif rainfed; summer crop needs irrigation every 7–10 days; critical at flowering & pod fill.

Weeds, Pests & Diseases

  • Pests: aphids, whitefly, pod borer; Diseases: YMV, powdery mildew, Cercospora.

Harvest & Yield

  • Pick at ~80% pod maturity (multiple pickings); 0.8–1.2 t/ha rainfed; up to 1.5 t/ha irrigated.

Black Gram (Vigna mungo)

Temp: 25–30°C Soil pH: 6.0–7.5 Short duration

Climate & Soil

  • Warm, humid conditions; loamy soils with good drainage.

Land Preparation

  • One plough + 2–3 harrowings to fine tilth.

Seed & Sowing

  • Row spacing 30–45 cm; plant 10–15 cm apart; depth 3–5 cm.

Nutrient Management

  • Starter N 15–20; P 40–50 kg/ha; Zn 5–10 kg/ha if deficient. Rhizobium inoculation.

Irrigation

  • Rainfed in monsoon; irrigate at flowering & pod fill in summer; avoid excess water.

Weeds, Pests & Diseases

  • Pests: pod borer, aphids, whitefly; Diseases: YMV, powdery mildew, Cercospora.

Harvest & Yield

  • Harvest when pods blacken; 1.0–1.3 t/ha rainfed; up to 1.5 t/ha irrigated.

Lentil (Lens culinaris)

Temp: 15–25°C Soil pH: 6.0–7.0 Cool-season

Climate & Soil

  • Cool, dry winter crop; avoid waterlogged fields.

Land Preparation

  • Deep plough + harrow; conserve soil moisture.

Seed & Sowing

  • Rows 20–30 cm; depth 3–4 cm; disease-resistant varieties.

Nutrient Management

  • Starter N 20–25; P 40–50 kg/ha; K only if deficient; Rhizobium inoculation.

Irrigation

  • Mostly rainfed; if possible irrigate at flowering & pod fill.

Weeds, Pests & Diseases

  • Pests: cutworms, aphids; Diseases: rust, wilt, root rot.

Pomegranate (Punica granatum)

Temp: Hot semi-arid Soil pH: 5.5–7.5 Drip ideal

Climate & Soil

  • Hot, semi-arid to subtropical; well-drained loams.

Land Preparation

  • Deep plough and level; add 10–20 t/ha FYM pre-plant.

Planting

  • Spacing 4–5 m; plant in monsoon (Jul–Aug) or spring (Feb–Mar).

Nutrient Management

  • Young: N 250–300 g, P 125–150 g, K 250–300 g/plant/yr; Mature: ~2×. Include Zn & B as needed.

Irrigation

  • Drip preferred; maintain moisture but avoid waterlogging. Critical at flowering, fruit set & development.

Weeds, Pests & Diseases

  • Pests: fruit borer, aphids, mealybugs; Diseases: bacterial blight, fruit rot. Prune for airflow.

Harvest & Yield

  • Harvest 5–7 months after flowering at full color; 10–12 t/ha typical.

Banana (Musa spp.)

Temp: 25–35°C Soil pH: 6.0–7.5 High humidity

Climate & Soil

  • Tropical; deep loams rich in organic matter; no frost or waterlogging.

Land Preparation

  • Pits 60×60×60 cm with compost mix; raised beds in heavy soils.

Planting

  • Spacing 1.8×1.5 m (dwarf) or 2×2 m (tall). Healthy suckers/tissue culture plants.

Nutrient Management

  • N 200–400 g, P 50–60 g, K 300–400 g per plant/year in 4–6 splits + 10–15 kg FYM.

Irrigation

  • Regular irrigation: 7–8 days (winter), 4–5 days (summer); drip ideal.

Weeds, Pests & Diseases

  • Pests: weevil, aphids, nematodes; Diseases: Panama wilt, Sigatoka, bunchy top.

Harvest & Yield

  • Harvest 11–12 months after planting; 40–50 t/ha common.

Mango (Mangifera indica)

Temp: 24–30°C Soil pH: 5.5–7.5 Dry flowering

Climate & Soil

  • Subtropical/tropical; dry weather at flowering; deep well-drained loams.

Land Preparation

  • Pits 1×1×1 m with 20–25 kg FYM.

Planting

  • Spacing 9–12 m; plant in monsoon or spring.

Nutrient Management

  • Young: 100 g N, 50 g P, 100 g K; Mature: 500 g N, 250 g P, 500 g K per tree/year in two splits.

Irrigation

  • Weekly for young trees; irrigate at fruit set & development; avoid excess at flowering.

Weeds, Pests & Diseases

  • Pests: hoppers, mealybugs, fruit fly; Diseases: powdery mildew, anthracnose.

Harvest & Yield

  • Harvest at full maturity (90–120 days after set); 8–10 t/ha (traditional) to 15–20 t/ha (high-density).

Grapes (Vitis vinifera)

Temp: 15–35°C Soil pH: 6.5–7.5 Low humidity at ripening

Climate & Soil

  • Warm, dry; deep, well-drained sandy loams preferred.

Land Preparation

  • Deep plough; 20–25 t/ha FYM; trellis/bower systems mandatory.

Planting

  • Rows 2–3 m; vines 1.5–2 m apart; train & prune for canopy and airflow.

Nutrient Management

  • N 400–500 g, P 200–250 g, K 400–500 g per vine/year; add Mg & Zn as needed.

Irrigation

  • Every 7–10 days in summer; reduce near ripening for sweetness.

Weeds, Pests & Diseases

  • Pests: mealybugs, thrips; Diseases: downy & powdery mildew, anthracnose.

Harvest & Yield

  • Harvest at 18–22° Brix; 20–25 t/ha typical.

Watermelon (Citrullus lanatus)

Temp: 22–35°C Soil pH: 6.0–7.5 Sandy loam

Climate & Soil

  • Warm with long frost-free season; well-drained sandy loams.

Land Preparation

  • Ridges/raised beds; add 15–20 t/ha FYM.

Planting

  • Rows 3–4 m; plants 0.5–1 m; Feb–Mar (summer), Nov–Dec (spring).

Nutrient Management

  • N 80–100, P 40–50, K 40–50 kg/ha; split doses for quality and firmness.

Irrigation

  • Every 5–7 days early; reduce near harvest to improve sweetness.

Weeds, Pests & Diseases

  • Pests: aphids, fruit flies, red pumpkin beetle; Diseases: Fusarium wilt, powdery mildew.

Harvest & Yield

  • Harvest at tendril-dry & ground-spot yellow; 20–30 t/ha common.

Muskmelon (Cucumis melo)

Temp: 25–30°C Soil pH: 6.0–7.5 Warm & dry

Climate & Soil

  • Warm, low humidity at ripening; sandy loams preferred.

Land Preparation

  • Raised beds; 15–20 t/ha FYM.

Planting

  • Rows 1.5–2 m; plants 0.5–0.75 m; Feb–Mar or Oct–Nov.

Nutrient Management

  • N 60–80, P 40–50, K 40–50 kg/ha; half N + full P&K at sowing; rest N at vine growth.

Irrigation

  • Every 5–6 days initially; reduce near harvest.

Weeds, Pests & Diseases

  • Pests: aphids, fruit flies, red pumpkin beetle; Diseases: powdery/downy mildew, gummy stem blight.

Harvest & Yield

  • Harvest at netting & aroma stage; 15–20 t/ha typical.

Apple (Malus domestica)

Chilling: 1000–1500 h Soil pH: 6.0–7.0 Slope sites preferred

Climate & Soil

  • Temperate with adequate chill; well-drained loams.

Land Preparation

  • Pits 1×1×1 m with 40–50 kg FYM; choose gentle slopes to avoid frost pockets.

Planting

  • Spacing 4×4 to 6×6 m depending on rootstock; plant in dormancy (Dec–Feb).

Nutrient Management

  • Young: N 70–150 g, P 35–70 g, K 70–150 g; Mature: 500–700 g N/tree; split pre-bloom/post-set/post-harvest.

Irrigation

  • 7–10 days in summer for young orchards; irrigate during fruit development.

Weeds, Pests & Diseases

  • Pests: codling moth, aphids; Diseases: scab, powdery mildew, fire blight.

Harvest & Yield

  • Seeds brown & color developed; 8–12 t/ha traditional; 25–30 t/ha high-density systems.

Orange (Citrus sinensis)

Temp: 12–35°C Soil pH: 5.5–7.5 Well-drained soils

Climate & Soil

  • Subtropical/tropical; sandy loams preferred.

Land Preparation

  • Pits 60×60×60 cm with FYM & topsoil.

Planting

  • Spacing 6×6 m; plant in monsoon or spring.

Nutrient Management

  • Yr1: 100 g N, 40 g P, 40 g K; Mature: 600–800 g N, 200–300 g P, 400–600 g K per tree/year + micronutrients (Zn, Fe).

Irrigation

Weeds, Pests & Diseases

Harvest & Yield

Papaya (Carica papaya)

Temp: 22–33°C Soil pH: 6.0–6.5 Frost sensitive

Climate & Soil

  • Tropical; avoid waterlogging; well-drained loams.

Land Preparation

  • Pits 60×60×60 cm with FYM & topsoil; good drainage essential.

Planting

  • Spacing 2×2 m; healthy seedlings; plant in monsoon or spring.

Nutrient Management

  • N 250–300, P 150–200, K 250–300 g/plant/year in 4–5 splits + organics; micronutrients as needed.

Irrigation

  • Every 5–7 days; reduce near ripening to improve quality.

Weeds, Pests & Diseases

  • Pests: aphids, fruit fly, mites; Diseases: PRSV (ringspot), powdery mildew, root rot.

Harvest & Yield

  • Harvest at yellowing skin; 40–60 t/ha/year typical.

Coconut (Cocos nucifera)

Temp: 27–32°C Soil pH: 5.2–8.0 Humid tropics

Climate & Soil

  • Humid tropics; rainfall >1500 mm; sandy loams, coastal belts suitable.

Land Preparation

  • Pits 1×1×1 m with 15–20 kg FYM; windbreaks recommended.

Planting

  • Spacing 7.5×7.5 m; plant at monsoon onset.

Nutrient Management

  • N 500 g, P 320 g, K 1200 g per palm/year + organics; split applications.

Irrigation

  • Every 5–10 days in dry months; drip highly beneficial.

Weeds, Pests & Diseases

  • Pests: rhinoceros beetle, red palm weevil, eriophyid mite; Diseases: bud rot, stem bleeding.

Harvest & Yield

  • Harvest every 45–60 days; 80–100 nuts/palm/year typical.

Cotton (Gossypium spp.)

Temp: 21–30°C Soil pH: 6.0–8.0 Vertisols ideal

Climate & Soil

  • Warm climate with 150–200 frost-free days; deep black soils preferred.

Land Preparation

  • Deep plough + harrow; incorporate 15–20 t/ha FYM.

Planting

  • Spacing 75×30 cm (compact) or 90×45 cm (hybrids); sow Apr–May (irrigated) / Jun–Jul (rainfed).

Nutrient Management

  • N 80–100, P 40–50, K 40–50 kg/ha; half N + full P&K at sowing, rest N at flowering.

Irrigation

  • Every 6–8 days in summer; critical at flowering & boll formation.

Weeds, Pests & Diseases

  • Pests: bollworms, aphids, whiteflies; Diseases: bacterial blight, wilt, leaf curl virus. Bt cotton helps with bollworms.

Harvest & Yield

  • Pick when bolls open fully; 15–25 q/ha lint in irrigated systems.

Jute (Corchorus spp.)

Temp: 24–37°C Soil pH: 6.0–7.0 High rainfall

Climate & Soil

  • Warm, humid with 1500–2000 mm rainfall; fertile alluvial soils.

Land Preparation

  • 2–3 ploughings to fine tilth; incorporate 5–10 t/ha FYM; ensure drainage.

Planting

  • Sow Mar–Apr; seed rate 5–7 kg/ha (C. capsularis) or 7–8 kg/ha (C. olitorius); spacing 30×15 cm.

Nutrient Management

  • N 40–60, P 20–30, K 40–50 kg/ha; half N + full P&K at sowing; rest N at 45 DAS.

Irrigation

  • Mostly rainfed; irrigate during dry spells; avoid waterlogging.

Weeds, Pests & Diseases

  • Pests: stem weevil, semilooper; Diseases: stem/soft rot, anthracnose; rotate crops and use resistant varieties.

Harvest, Retting & Yield

  • Harvest at early flowering (90–120 days) for best fiber; rett for 2–3 weeks; 20–30 q fiber/ha typical.

Coffee (Coffea spp.)

Temp: 15–28°C Soil pH: 6.0–6.5 Shade-grown

Climate & Soil

  • Tropical highlands with 1500–2500 mm rain; humus-rich, well-drained loams; partial shade required.

Land Preparation

  • Maintain shade trees; pits 45×45×45 cm with composted soil.

Planting

  • Spacing 2×2 m (Arabica) or 2.5×2.5 m (Robusta); plant at monsoon onset.

Nutrient Management

  • N 120–140, P 60–80, K 120–140 kg/ha/year in 3–4 splits + organics; include Mg, Zn, B if deficient.

Irrigation

  • Supplement in dry months; key irrigations: blossom (pre-flowering) and backing (post-flowering).

Weeds, Pests & Diseases

  • Pests: coffee berry borer, mealybugs, stem borers; Diseases: leaf rust, berry disease, root rots. IPM essential.

Harvest, Processing & Yield

  • Hand-pick ripe red cherries; multiple pickings; process via wet (washed) or dry (natural) methods; Arabica 600–900 kg/ha, Robusta 900–1200 kg/ha clean coffee.
